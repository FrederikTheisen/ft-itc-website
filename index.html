02 / Method notes

Making the Spolar–Record approach easier to inspect.

The Spolar–Record thermodynamic approach connects temperature-dependent binding data to the entropy of conformational change and the number of residues that fold upon binding. FT-ITC Analysis makes that chain of reasoning available as a practical, inspectable workflow.

ΔSconf=ΔSbind − ΔShydr − ΔSrt

The exact treatment, assumptions and parameter choices belong with the paper and the data—not hidden behind a single number.
