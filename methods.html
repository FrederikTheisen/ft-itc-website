Advanced analyses

What changes when binding happens?

When an experiment series is designed for it, FT-ITC Analysis supports additional views of the physical processes behind binding.

01

Conformational & structuring analysis

Use temperature-dependent binding thermodynamics to examine conformational entropy and the structural change associated with binding. This specialised workflow builds on the Spolar-Record approach and belongs with its assumptions, data and literature context.

02

Salt dependence

Evaluate ionic-strength effects and estimate the selected model's extrapolated dissociation constant at zero ionic strength.

03

Proton linkage

Compare titrations across buffers with different protonation enthalpies to investigate linked protonation changes.
