Advanced method note

Spolar–Record, in context.

This is a specialised thermodynamic interpretation for particular experimental questions—not a required step in a standard FT-ITC Analysis workflow.

What it asks

How much conformational change accompanies binding?

The Spolar–Record approach relates temperature-dependent binding thermodynamics to conformational entropy and estimates of residues folding upon binding. FT-ITC Analysis provides a practical workflow for examining this chain of reasoning alongside the underlying experimental analysis.

Interpretation depends on the assumptions, parameter choices and appropriate supporting evidence for a given system. Read the source literature alongside any software output.

Conceptual relationshipΔSconf = ΔSbind − ΔShydr − ΔSrt

Method-specific terms and assumptions require careful literature-based treatment.
