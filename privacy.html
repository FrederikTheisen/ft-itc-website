Registered access

Privacy,
plainly stated.

Version 2026-01 · Effective 16 September 2026

This notice explains what information is used when you request registered access to the FT-ITC Analysis web viewer and why it is needed.

1. Identity and registration data

We receive the name, email address and optional organisation that you submit. We use these details to assess and create registered access, prevent abuse, deliver access instructions and provide support.

2. Consent evidence

We keep evidence of the Terms and Privacy versions you accepted or acknowledged, together with the relevant registration event. This lets us demonstrate which documents applied at the time.

3. Security and Turnstile

Cloudflare Turnstile is used to help distinguish genuine registration requests from automated abuse. The browser sends the challenge token to the registration service; the website does not receive or store the Turnstile secret.

4. Email delivery

Access emails are delivered using Resend. The delivery provider may process the recipient address and message-delivery information as needed to send the email and maintain delivery reliability.

5. Retention

Registration and consent records are retained only as long as needed for account administration, security, legal obligations and support. Operational logs may be kept for a shorter period for abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

6. Deletion and correction

To ask for correction or deletion of your registration information, contact support@ft-itc.org. We may need to verify the request and may retain limited information where required for security or legal reasons.

7. Operator-account linkage

Registered access may be linked to an operator-managed account or service record so that quotas, access status and support can be administered. This linkage does not give the website access to bearer-code secrets.

8. Contact

Questions about this notice or your information can be sent to support@ft-itc.org.
